These clips are a fixed 32-utterance slice of LibriSpeech test-clean,
published by OpenSLR at https://www.openslr.org/12 under the Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

LibriSpeech is described in:

  V. Panayotov, G. Chen, D. Povey and S. Khudanpur, "LibriSpeech: An ASR
  corpus based on public domain audio books", ICASSP 2015.
