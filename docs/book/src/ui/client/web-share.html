the share sheet

The page a share opens: the files another app handed forge, the seat they go to, and the words that ride with them. One page of its own - the hardware Back closes it through the same history every page uses. Every control is a 44px target on a finger, nothing exists only on hover, the seat is chosen from a sheet the page draws rather than the platform's own chooser, and the box carries the composer's own dictation.
